Separately installed dependencies
--------------------------------
The source release contains dependency declarations, not the installed runtime
or parser image. Installation obtains components under their own terms, including:

- python-docx 1.2.0, lxml 6.1.3 and python-telegram-bot 22.8, declared in
  forsic_plugin/plugin.yaml and requirements-forsic.txt.
- dissect.target 3.25.1 and dissect.xfs 3.13 in the Python 3.12-based parser image,
  declared in Dockerfile.tools.
- duckdb 1.5.6 (MIT), wheel hashes in requirements-analysis.txt.
- python-docx 1.2.0, lxml 6.1.3 and typing-extensions 4.15.0, pinned wheel
  hashes in requirements-analysis.txt; installed Python distribution notices
  remain included. These also support derived documents inside the analysis image.
- Headless LibreOffice Writer, Noto CJK fonts and Poppler utilities from the
  signed Ubuntu repositories, exact direct package versions in Dockerfile.analysis.
  Their upstream and bundled-component notices remain under /usr/share/doc/ in
  the image; retain those notices when distributing the built runtime.
- Sleuth Kit (Ubuntu sleuthkit 4.12.1+dfsg-3build1), TestDisk/PhotoRec
  (testdisk 7.2-0.1build1) and libewf utilities (ewf-tools 20140816-2build1),
  installed from signed Ubuntu repositories at the direct versions declared in
  Dockerfile.analysis. Preserve their upstream and bundled-component copyright
  and license files under /usr/share/doc/, including dependencies' notices.
  Their licenses are independent of Forsic's; retain the package/source provenance
  and applicable source-distribution obligations when distributing the runtime.
  Upstream projects: https://www.sleuthkit.org/ ; https://www.cgsecurity.org/ ;
  https://github.com/libyal/libewf .
- Hayabusa 4.2.0 (AGPL-3.0-only), bundled rules (DRL-1.1 plus individual notices),
  and YARA-X 1.21.0 (BSD-3-Clause), downloaded separately using the asset/license
  hashes and source links in tools/forensic-tools.lock.json. The installed image
  retains license files and per-rule/config hashes under /opt/forensic-tools/.
- Gitleaks 8.30.1 CLI and its exact default rule snapshot (MIT), from
  https://github.com/gitleaks/gitleaks/tree/v8.30.1 . Archive, extracted executable,
  rules and original LICENSE hashes are pinned in tools/forensic-tools.lock.json.
  The official README checked on 2026-10-20 states it is feature complete and
  future releases will be security patches. No Betterleaks or online credential
  verifier is included. Preserve the CLI's bundled Go dependency notices too.
- Presidio analyzer 2.2.364 (MIT), originally Microsoft, now redirected upstream
  https://github.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/tree/2.2.364 , and regex
  2026.9.29 (Apache-2.0 AND CNRI-Python). Exact CPython 3.12 Linux wheel hashes
  appear in requirements-sensitive.txt and tools/forensic-tools.lock.json.
  Original wheel license/distribution metadata and Presidio LICENSE are retained
  under /opt/forensic-tools/sensitive/python and /opt/forensic-tools/licenses.
  The dedicated helper loads only hash-checked, unmodified Presidio core and
  selected Korean/card recognizer modules. Its minimal package initializer and
  local email/Korean phone format patterns are Forsic adaptations, not upstream
  recognizers or a supported full Presidio installation. The default analyzer,
  NLP dependencies, language models, registry and remote recognizers are not
  installed or initialized. No model quality or complete Korean PII coverage is
  implied by this deliberately restricted installation.
- Plaso 20260720 (Apache-2.0) and its native dependencies/base operating system,
  from the official image digest in Dockerfile.analysis. Existing notices in
  that image remain installed; their licenses are not replaced by Forsic's.
- DFIQ (Apache-2.0), pinned question metadata and original license retained under
  forsic_plugin/skills/investigation-recipes/references/. Local procedural
  summaries are independently authored and are not official DFIQ approaches.
- Hermes-managed Python/Node tools and transitive packages, identified by the
  pinned PM, Python and frontend lockfiles under upstream/hermes/.
- Host utilities such as libewf, Docker and their dependencies, installed by the
  operator rather than included as Forsic binaries.

Consult each installed distribution's license and notices, including those of
bundled libraries and container base packages. When distributing a built runtime
or image, retain the notices and satisfy any additional obligations for the actual
components included; the Forsic MIT license is not a substitute for those terms.
This summary is not a full software bill of materials or a license compatibility
certification for every possible installation.
