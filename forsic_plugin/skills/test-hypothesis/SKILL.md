# Evaluate hypotheses and choose the next check

Briefly state the favored explanation and realistic competing explanations that could overturn it. Do not enumerate
every imaginable alternative. Compare key observations, sources, inferred links, and counterarguments to choose
the current answer. Analytical judgment remains possible without direct evidence. '관측 확인', '개연성 높은 해석',
and '서로 구별 어려움' have different meanings.

## Confidence and investigation value

Explain qualitative confidence through source quality, collection scope, independence of clues, explanatory power
relative to alternatives, and conflicting evidence. Do not use uncalibrated probabilities or counts of circumstances.
Summaries, notes, and reports derived from the same original are not independent evidence. Even observations
compatible with benign and malicious activity can be compared in context for which explanation predicts them more
naturally. Results similarly expected under both do not improve ranking. Mark links without direct records as inference.

Confidence is separate from investigation priority. Actively investigate weak leads when impactful or inexpensive
to check. Before execution, briefly decide how each possible result would change the current answer or next action.
Choose checks that distinguish alternatives or narrow scope. Do not investigate every lead to the same depth.

## Meaning of results and gaps

Assess command/configuration presence, execution, successful effects, actor identity, and authorization separately.
An observation at one stage does not automatically establish the others. Multiple independent circumstances can
support a favored inference, but do not invent unobserved attributes, times, outputs, or identities. Names and
familiar paths alone do not verify function or authorization.

Evaluate empty results within the tested targets, periods, expressions, remaining pages, and recording capability.
For missing logs, first examine whether they were generated, acquisition scope, and normal rotation/retention.
Selective gaps combined with relevant settings and other surviving traces can change hypothesis ranking. Failure
to find a file alone establishes neither past existence, deletion, nor concealment intent. Record gaps neither
refute nor automatically prove attack. If further recovery is difficult, switch to other discriminating evidence.

Compare results with existing counterevidence to update the answer, confidence, and next check. Reuse successful
results and originals. Reduce repeated searches that do not change judgment. If a benign explanation is sufficient
and remaining checks have little value, close the defined scope under forensic-investigation. Insufficient or
unreviewed evidence differs from a benign verdict. Revisit closed scope when new counterevidence appears.
Closing a hypothesis does not delete originals.
