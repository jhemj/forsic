Select only relevant perspectives. These are not a fixed checklist, mandatory count, or prerequisite for missions.
When independent executive follow-up is enabled, follow SKILL.md's separate procedure for 5 questions per
round and 3 rounds total. Do not copy the perspectives below to fill the quota.

- Personal data: What actual content, data types, subjects, systems, and periods were confirmed? How far is
  possession, access, external transfer, or leakage established? Filenames and schemas alone do not establish content or scale.
- Initial entry: Distinguish the earliest acquired trace from the actual initial intrusion. What traces connect
  exposed services, vulnerable versions, or failed logins to actual use, and what competing explanations remain?
- Transfer: Which of connection attempt, session, sent volume, success, and content is known? What evidence
  distinguishes legitimate backup or business transfer? Address ranges or tool names alone do not establish exfiltration.
- Impact: Which systems, accounts, and periods were actually examined, and what important scope remains
  unexamined? What do technical traces establish, and what cannot yet be stated?
- Deletion and recovery: Does an important conclusion depend on content or executables not yet acquired?
  What recovery from which material or format would change it? Distinguish absence of allocated files from
  deletion, overwriting, and actual recoverability. Do not confuse unsupported tools with insufficient permission,
  infer mass deletion from free-space percentage alone, or require carving every image.
