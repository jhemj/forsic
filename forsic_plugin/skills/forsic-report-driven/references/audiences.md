# Executive body and appendix in the same document

Share the same current answers, evidence numbers, important counterarguments, and limitations in one integrated report.

Use natural narrative headings such as 「조사 결과 종합」, 「유입 및 행위 양상」, 「정보 영향 및 확인 근거」,
「침해 판단 종합」, and 「주요 사건 경과」. Do not list the seven assessment dimensions as Q&A.
Distinguish personal-data possession from possible external access and actual transfer; first observation
from first compromise; and commands from successful execution and content leakage. Keep core uncertainty
in the relevant sentence. Do not create separate body chapters for generic limitations or evidence integrity.
Keep priority response actions and detailed investigation steps in the practitioner appendix, outside the main body.

The body must stand alone for a nontechnical executive. Lead with the affected system, discovered facts,
why the combined facts matter and established impact. Explain unfamiliar terms beside first use.
The overall cell uses a headline followed by a few complete nominal sentences; major-finding explanations connect
evidence to meaning rather than repeating the gauge label. Keep exact technical assumptions in the appendix.
Do not infer business interruption, confirmed unauthorized control or loss from suspicious artifacts alone.

Immediately after the headline, `synthesis` explains the combined observations, their meaning and why the
leading interpretation is favored. Keep a meaningful competing account here when it changes how the incident
is understood. Use connected, complete nominal-Korean sentences: observation → significance → supported
interpretation. A date-unknown observation may still explain the case; do not invent its place in the chronology.
Only work procedures, exact technical derivation and detailed scope move to `cell_details` in the appendix.
Neither a list of facts nor a list of unknowns replaces this explanation. Avoid repeating the headline in synthesis.

Write individual timeline observations first, then read the entire chronology page and complete
`timeline_summary` with its progression and significance. Each event interpretation explains what
the recorded action demonstrates in plain language. For example, successful authentication describes
accepted account access, while a later privileged-command record describes a different level of
activity; neither is automatically hostile. Link only supported actors, objects or operations.
Parallel observations can be explained together without asserting causation. Keep the main account
focused on meaning and place detailed limitations in the appendix. This is part of the existing
reader-order check and single copyedit pass, not another review round.

Web reading always shows the body, practitioner appendix and audit records in sequence. Reading
does not hide sections when a print scope is selected. Print controls default to the complete report;
readers may choose the body or body plus practitioner appendix for a shorter printout.

Aim for about 3 body pages; this is a soft target, never a reason to remove the explanation a reader needs. Assessment cards, 3–5 direct evidence items, or 5–8 important timeline
events are optional format examples, not mandatory counts or case evidence. Do not assign arbitrary dates
to activities whose times remain unknown.

In the same document's appendix, preserve full answers by question, qualitative confidence, actual observations,
full inference assumptions and competing explanations, important gaps, reopening conditions, exact originals and
coordinates, the full timeline, TZ and time-field meanings, tool failures and partial coverage, execution
arguments, receipts, hashes, the selected-evidence manifest, corrections, and permissions. Do not record full internal reasoning.

The user's existing executive question mode of 3 rounds × 5 questions is executive-followup's separate optional
path. Read-only gap review for the integrated report must not automatically create a fourth round or send additional questions.

### Context without an extra body chapter

Read the current body from an uninformed executive's perspective. Put decision-critical missing explanations
in the relevant existing finding. Do not create a separate 조사 기반·핵심 확인사항 body section.
Useful provenance, examination coverage and context belong in the practitioner appendix, using optional
`executive_context` only when another detail field does not already cover them. One to five source-bound
rows may be retained; absent context does not block completion. Do not insert a placeholder orientation row.

Distinguish physical-original custody, an acquired image, a verified analysis copy and a provider's statement.
A stored hash is not a completed comparison; historical observations do not establish current operating state.
Reuse existing findings and review answers. Do not invent business role, losses, quantities or completed actions.

separate review context: first read the executive body alone, then compare it with supporting material.
Check whether a first-time executive reader can understand the situation, significance, impact and decisions;
check sentence subjects, connections, terminology and repetition. Use at most three prioritized corrections.
Ren supplies exact existing text and a ready-to-use nominal-Korean replacement for useful wording changes.
Apply one targeted copyedit through the existing compose fields, preserving source bindings. If no useful
change is needed, retain the report. Keep minor preferences nonblocking. For the resulting version, use the
existing content/source freshness checks and final selection; do not start another general copyedit cycle,
