# Report evidence and shared copies

Look up body evidence numbers in the same bundle's appendix and selected-evidence.zip. Evidence cards link
the original claim, question, and version to exact excerpts and UTF-8 byte coordinates of selected preserved
returns and actual execution-start and return identifiers. Evidence counts do not score investigation completeness or conclusion confidence.

Character positions in preserved returns are not offsets in original disk images. Show original file, volume,
or record locations only where acquisition information exists. Analysis terminal output is derived material,
not examination of the entire original. A generated report establishes its wording, not the truth of that wording as original case evidence.

The bundle verifier checks hashes and locations of included files and excerpts. It cannot verify omitted
original images or full returns, or judge whether evidence logically entails a sentence. Leave unavailable
coordinates and times unknown. Do not derive deletion time solely from the last log or ctime. Present
reasonable time estimates with assumptions, evidence for each boundary, and competing explanations recorded in correlate-time.

Share only within approved recipients and material scope. The ZIP is for local download; do not add it to
existing automatic delivery of integrated HTML/Word reports. Do not attach an original-source ZIP to a redacted
report. A separate redacted copy requires an approved procedure distinguishing original preservation,
parent hash, redacted content, and copy hash.
